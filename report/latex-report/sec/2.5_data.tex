\section{Dataset}
\label{sec:data}

We evaluate our system on the StreetAware dataset~\cite{piadyk_2023_q1byv-qc065}, a high-resolution synchronized multimodal urban scene dataset captured at a real 4-corner traffic intersection in New York City. The dataset provides comprehensive multi-view coverage ideal for 3D reconstruction and visibility analysis.

\subsection{Camera Configuration}

The dataset features 8 synchronized RGB cameras mounted at 4 corner positions, with 2 cameras per corner oriented to capture different viewing angles. This configuration provides:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Full intersection coverage:} 360-degree visibility of all crosswalks and approaching traffic lanes
    \item \textbf{Overlapping fields of view:} Multiple cameras observe the same regions, enabling multi-view reconstruction and cross-camera track association
    \item \textbf{Diverse perspectives:} Each corner captures unique occlusion patterns and visibility conditions
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Scene Characteristics}

The intersection includes diverse static and dynamic elements:

\textbf{Static elements:} Road surfaces with lane markings, crosswalk stripes, sidewalks, curbs, traffic signals, street furniture (poles, signs, fire hydrants), building facades, and parked vehicles.

\textbf{Dynamic elements:} Moving vehicles (cars, trucks, buses, bicycles) and pedestrians crossing at various times and locations. The dataset captures typical urban intersection activity with multiple simultaneous objects.

\subsection{Data Processing Pipeline}

We process the raw video through the following stages:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Background extraction:} Temporal median filtering over 300 frames per camera to isolate static scene elements
    \item \textbf{Multi-view reconstruction:} Pi3 processing of 8 background images to establish world geometry and camera parameters
    \item \textbf{Per-frame detection:} YOLOv8x inference on all video frames for object detection
    \item \textbf{Tracking:} ByteTrack association to maintain object identities across frames
    \item \textbf{3D projection:} Ground plane intersection to localize objects in world coordinates
    \item \textbf{Multi-camera fusion:} Spatial-temporal clustering to merge tracks across views
\end{enumerate}
