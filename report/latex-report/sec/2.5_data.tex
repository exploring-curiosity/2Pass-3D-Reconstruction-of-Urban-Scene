\section{Data}
\label{sec:data}

We use the StreetAware dataset~\cite{piadyk_2023_q1byv-qc065}, a high-resolution synchronized multimodal urban scene dataset captured at a real 4-corner traffic intersection. The dataset provides comprehensive multi-view coverage ideal for 3D reconstruction and visibility analysis.

\subsection{Data Characteristics}
\textbf{Camera Setup:} 8 synchronized RGB cameras mounted at 4 corner positions (2 cameras per corner) provide full 360-degree coverage of the intersection. Each corner has cameras oriented to capture different viewing angles of the crosswalk area and approaching traffic lanes.

\textbf{Synchronization:} All cameras are hardware-synchronized to capture frames at the same timestamp, essential for multi-view 3D reconstruction. Frame rate is sufficient to track pedestrian and vehicle motion.

\textbf{Scene Complexity:} The intersection includes diverse elements: road surface, crosswalk markings, traffic signals, street furniture (poles, signs), parked vehicles, and moving traffic (cars, trucks, pedestrians, bicycles). This complexity makes it ideal for testing occlusion reasoning.

\textbf{Video Duration:} Sample sequences span typical intersection activity, capturing multiple pedestrians crossing and vehicles approaching from different directions.
