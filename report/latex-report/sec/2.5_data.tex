\section{\blue{Data}}
\label{sec:data}

\blue{We use the StreetAware dataset~\cite{piadyk_2023_q1byv-qc065}, a high-resolution synchronized multimodal urban scene dataset captured at a real 4-corner traffic intersection. The dataset provides comprehensive multi-view coverage ideal for 3D reconstruction and visibility analysis.}

\begin{figure}[h!]
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=0.8\linewidth]{assets/Camera_Placement.png}
    \caption{\blue{Camera placement indicated by green markers at the 4-corner traffic intersection}}
    \label{fig:camera_setup}
\end{figure}

\subsection{\blue{Data Characteristics}}
\blue{\textbf{Camera Setup:} 8 synchronized RGB cameras mounted at 4 corner positions (2 cameras per corner, forming stereo pairs) provide full 360-degree coverage of the intersection.}

\blue{\textbf{Synchronization:} All cameras are hardware-synchronized at 30 FPS, essential for stereo triangulation and multi-view 3D reconstruction.}

\blue{\textbf{Scene Complexity:} The intersection includes road surfaces, crosswalk markings, traffic signals, street furniture, parked vehicles, and moving traffic (cars, trucks, pedestrians, bicycles).}

\subsection{\blue{Data Processing}}
\blue{We extract static background frames via temporal median filtering over 300 frames to remove moving objects. For dynamic analysis, we leverage the stereo camera pairs at each corner for triangulation-based 3D localization, using Pi3-estimated camera parameters for projection.}
