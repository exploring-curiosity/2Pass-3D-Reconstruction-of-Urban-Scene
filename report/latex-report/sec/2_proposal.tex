\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\subsection{Intersection Safety Audits}
Traditional visibility and safety audits at intersections are done manually or in 2D. City planners and engineers typically analyze 2D video footage, static camera views, or CAD maps to perform LoS audits following guidelines like the AASHTO Green Book~\cite{aashto2018greenbook}. These methods cannot capture complex 3D occlusions from dynamic objects, leading to missed hazards. Recent work has shown that limited sight distance is a contributing factor in pedestrian fatalities~\cite{nhtsa2023pedestrian,lord2007safety}.

\subsection{Multi-View 3D Reconstruction}
Multi-view stereo (MVS) methods like MVSNet~\cite{yao2018mvsnet} estimate depth from multiple calibrated views. Recent advances include DUSt3R~\cite{dust3r}, which jointly estimates camera poses and dense geometry from uncalibrated images, making it suitable for outdoor scenes. Neural radiance fields (NeRF)~\cite{mildenhall2020nerf} enable novel view synthesis but require extensive optimization. We adopt DUSt3R for its balance of quality and practicality in outdoor multi-view scenarios.

\subsection{Object Detection and Tracking}
Modern object detectors like YOLO~\cite{redmon2016yolo} and its successors (YOLOv8~\cite{yolov8}) achieve real-time performance on pedestrian and vehicle detection. Multi-object tracking (MOT) methods maintain object identities across frames. ByteTrack~\cite{bytetrack} uses a simple yet effective strategy of associating every detection box, including low-confidence ones, achieving state-of-the-art tracking performance. For multi-camera scenarios, tracks must be associated across views using spatial and temporal cues.

\subsection{Our Contribution}
We combine multi-view static reconstruction (DUSt3R) with per-camera dynamic tracking (YOLOv8x + ByteTrack) and multi-camera association to create a unified 3D scene representation for LoS audits. Unlike prior work that focuses on either static reconstruction or dynamic tracking, we explicitly separate and fuse both for accurate visibility analysis.
