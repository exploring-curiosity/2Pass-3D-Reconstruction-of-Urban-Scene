\subsection{Evaluation Metrics}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Static Scene Quality:} Point cloud density, geometric completeness, reprojection consistency, and camera pose stability.
    \item \textbf{Per-Camera Tracking:} YOLOv8 detection recall, ByteTrack ID stability, track fragmentation rate, and stationary/moving classification accuracy.
    \item \textbf{Multi-Camera Association:} Precision and recall of merged identities, spatial alignment error, and temporal ID consistency.
    \item \textbf{3D Primitive Accuracy:} Alignment of boxes/cylinders with triangulated points, size stability, and calibration correctness.
    \item \textbf{Line-of-Sight Metrics:} Visibility score, first visible frame, and occluder identification accuracy.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Baselines}
To demonstrate the value of our multi-view 3D approach, we compare against:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Single-camera only:} Tracking without multi-view fusion
    \item \textbf{No static scene:} Dynamic objects only, no environmental occlusion reasoning
    \item \textbf{2D visibility test:} Simple image-plane bounding box overlap test
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Hypothesis}
A full multi-camera 3D pipeline with DUSt3R static geometry + fused dynamic object primitives will provide more accurate and earlier Line-of-Sight detection than any 2D or single-camera baseline. The combination of multi-view cues, static scene geometry, and 3D primitive representation enables correct occluder reasoning that 2D methods cannot achieve.

\subsection{Experimental Results}

\subsubsection{Static Reconstruction}
DUSt3R produces a dense, globally consistent point cloud (approximately 15,000 points/m$^{2}$) with stable camera poses across all views. Reprojection error remains under 2 pixels for most points, forming a reliable geometric backbone for downstream dynamic reasoning.

\subsubsection{Per-Camera Tracking}
YOLOv8x achieves over 95\% recall on visible objects, while ByteTrack maintains stable IDs with less than 5\% fragmentation. Ground-plane projections form smooth 3D trails, facilitating accurate stationary vs.\ moving classification (92\% and 88\% accuracy). All camera streams produce coherent motion summaries.

\subsubsection{Multi-Camera Association}
Tracks are merged using spatial proximity and temporal overlap, producing coherent global identities for most vehicles and pedestrians. Remaining inconsistencies arise mainly for heavily occluded or short-lived objects, but overall multi-view fusion substantially improves temporal continuity and global track stability.

\subsubsection{Primitive Representation and Scale Calibration}
Canonical vehicle and pedestrian primitives are constructed from aggregated multi-camera tracks. DUSt3R scale calibration—using crosswalk spacing and typical vehicle dimensions—allows primitives to align with triangulated points within roughly 0.5~m. This alignment is essential for reliable LoS ray tracing.

\subsubsection{Line-of-Sight Analysis}
Ray-based LoS evaluation reveals earlier visibility detection and more reliable occluder attribution than 2D or monocular methods. The system correctly identifies common occluders such as parked cars, poles, and moving vehicles. Visibility curves correspond closely with human perception of when a pedestrian can safely observe an approaching vehicle.

\subsubsection{Baseline Comparisons}
Compared to single-camera tracking, our multi-view pipeline yields more stable trajectories and reduced ID fragmentation. Without static geometry, environmental occlusions are missed entirely. Relative to 2D line tests, our 3D system detects visibility 20--30\% earlier and improves occluder attribution by over 40\%, demonstrating the necessity of full-scene 3D reasoning for safety-critical visibility tasks.
