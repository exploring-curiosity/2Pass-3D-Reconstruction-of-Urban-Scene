\begin{abstract}
We present a two-pass 3D reconstruction system for line-of-sight audits at urban intersections. Our method processes synchronized multi-camera video to reconstruct static scene geometry using Pi3 and tracks dynamic objects per-camera using YOLOv8x and ByteTrack, with stereo triangulation for 3D localization. We propose multi-camera association via clustering and canonical 3D primitive representation (oriented bounding boxes for vehicles, cylinders for pedestrians) to enable ray-casting visibility analysis that can determine when pedestrians can see approaching vehicles and what occluders block their view. Preliminary results show successful static reconstruction and per-camera tracking across 8 synchronized views at a real intersection.
\end{abstract}
